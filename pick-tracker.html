What this tracker counts

One pick = one player in one game. The same player stays separate across different weeks. Model values appear side by side inside that pick; missing model forecasts are not invented.

Frozen, not rewritten. Each model's first eligible forecast is saved before kickoff. Open a pick to see every statistic, its capture time and forecast fingerprint.

Errors, not artificial wins. Error is the absolute difference from the actual stat. Above/below describes the result versus the forecast, not betting advice. Missing stats stay ungraded. Decimal TDs are expected counts, not scoring probabilities.

Historical picks without a saved numerical forecast are not backfilled here. Earlier threshold-based results remain on the homepage.
