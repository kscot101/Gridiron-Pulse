Settings were chosen using an earlier season, not this test year. The evaluation includes players with a recorded game and at least two earlier appearances for that team. It does not test whether a player will be active, future injuries, or every possible roster change. These results are not a live win rate.

What the trial changes

Quarterbacks: expected team attempts, the quarterback's share of those attempts, and passing efficiency. Running backs: expected carries and rushing efficiency, plus a separate receiving estimate. Receivers and tight ends: expected team attempts, target share, receiving efficiency and catch rate.

Player and team usage come from earlier completed games. Smaller efficiency samples are blended with position-level historical averages using settings selected on the validation season. Touchdowns use their own scoring-per-opportunity estimates, not the yardage multiplier.

TD decimals are expected counts, not scoring probabilities. A projection of 0.6 touchdowns is not a 60% chance of scoring. The current trial does not claim to incorporate route participation, snap forecasts, red-zone tracking or opponent-adjusted coverage. Positions are resolved with athlete IDs and roster data; a missing identity is not guessed from a receiving-stat category.

The original Player Edge HIT/MISS record remains available on the homepage as a legacy threshold-based record. It is not combined with these exact-stat errors. All projections are estimates conditional on playing, not guarantees or betting recommendations.
